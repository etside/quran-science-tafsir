Introduction

The Surah talks about the Journey to Hell.

Tafsir of the Surah The ―Noise and Clamor.‖ What is the ―Noise and Clamor‖? And what will explain to thee what the ―Noise and Clamor‖ is? A Day whereon men will be like moths scattered about, and the mountains will be like carded wool.

Remarks:

The Surah depicts the final event. The Land of Judgment will be created from the matter taken out of the Thaqal (the revived universe, halted in the state of heavy mass). After the Judgment, those destined for Jannah will proceed along As-Sirat leading to Paradise. Meanwhile, those destined for Hell will be cast into the Thaqal, unrolling violently at that time. Soon the Land of Judgment will be grasped by the rolling Thaqal that will be reviving violently as the Skies. The Land of Judgment will then explode into pieces. The sinners, moving toward Hell on their faces (flying through space), will appear like moths scattered in all directions; while the shattered fragments of the Land of Judgment will resemble carded wool. Eventually, each human will be drawn into the object (galaxy), determined.

Then, he whose balance is heavy will be in a life of good pleasure and satisfaction.

Remarks:

By that time, the people of Jannah will have reached their destinations; they will be in the state of pleasure and contentment.

But, he whose balance is light, his mother will be endless hole. And what will explain to thee what this is? A fire blazing fiercely!

Remarks

The Thaqal, rolling into the Samawaat, will release super- compact galaxies in its initial state. Each galaxy will pull the sinner destined for it. However, the galaxies will take some time to form stars and planets. Initially, a sinner will be drawn into the super-massive black hole. As the verse says: ―But, he whose balance is light, his mother will be endless hole.‖ Here, the 'endless hole' refers to the 'super-massive black hole.' This black hole will pull enough matter from the Thaqal to eventually form stars and planets over time. There is no chance of missing the destination, as the driving angels will be assigned from the moment of resurrection: ―And there will come forth every soul; with each will be an (angel) to drive and an (angel) to bear witness (Amal-Nama)‖ [Al Quran 50:21]
