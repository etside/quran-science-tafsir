Introduction

The Surah speaks about the revelation of the Quran. It discusses the Command System of the Most High and calls for absolute devotion. None can intercede in the materialization of His plan. He is the sole authority to forgive and to save from disease, danger, and Hell.

Tafsir of the Surah Section 1 of Chapter 53 [Verse 1-12]: The Descent of the Quran

By the star when it goes down, your companion is neither astray, nor being misled, nor does he say of desire; it is no less than inspiration sent down to him. He was taught by one (Gabriel), intense in strength, one of soundness. And he rose to true shape while he was in the higher horizon. Then he approached and descended and was at a distance of but two bow-lengths, or nearer. So, did convey the inspiration to His Servant what He to convey. The heart in no way falsified that which he saw. Will ye then dispute with him concerning what he saw?

Remarks

The Quran descended in the First Sky at one time, but it was revealed to Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) in small parts. When the first few verses were revealed in the Cave of Hira, Gabriel appeared as a massive entity on the horizon. Gradually, he approached and shrank in size, until he was right in front of Prophet Muhammad (pbuh). Gabriel showed the verses, written on something, and asked Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) to read. However, he could not read. Gabriel embraced him and opened a path through his backbone to implant the verses directly into his brain. The entry point of this path appeared as a swollen muscle on his backbone, just below the neck. This entry point is called Mohr-e-Nabuat. Initially, Gabriel implanted the database (dhikra) of the Quran into the brain of Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) so that the verses could be stored in their proper places whenever they would be given. After inserting the database, Gabriel inserted the first few verses of Surah Al Alaq. The verses used to come as ruh, created with low- energy electromagnetic force fields, functioning as brain data. Therefore, it was not possible for Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) to forget a verse, and he was told not to be anxious about remembering. Memorizing the verses was not needed for him. Whenever Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) wished, the data of the verses would be downloaded from his brain into his mind (qalb), and he could recite them. Subsequently, the minor angels of Gabriel delivered the verses through this path directly into his brain.

Section 2 of Chapter 53 [Verse 13-18]: Sidratul-Muntaha

And certainly, he saw him in Second Descent near Lote-Tree (Sidratul-Muntaha), beyond which none may pass; near it is Jannaatu-Mawa. Covered the Sidratul-Muntaha that which cover; sight never swerved, nor did it go wrong, for truly, did he see of the signs of his Lord, the Greatest!

Remarks:

The 'Second Descent' mentioned in the above verses refers to the Night Journey (Miraj) when Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) visited Samawaat, Araf, Arsh, and Jannaat. To understand the Second Descent, one needs a general understanding of the entire creation.

Sidratul-Muntaha

During the Night Journey, Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) saw Sidratul-Muntaha from the Seventh Sky (the Outermost Sky). It resembled a giant upside-down lote tree, rooted in the Arsh and hanging over the Araf (Elevated Land) with two main branches. One branch connects the Eastern Araf, while the other connects the Western Araf. It is somewhat like the Azadi Tower of Tehran, but hanging from above. FIGURE 53.1: Azadi Tower, Tehran

The above verses say: "…beyond which none may pass; near it is Jannat al-Mawa." If a person enters Sidratul-Muntaha through the Eastern Branch and ascends, he will reach the Arsh; and if he enters the Western Branch and proceeds, he reaches the Western Araf. The Western Araf lies at the mid-level of Jannaat; it is the level of Jannat al-Mawa. The Sidratul-Muntaha acts as a checkpoint, preventing anyone from the Eastern Araf entering the Arsh or Western Araf without permission.

The Barzakh is a barrier space through which only light can pass. It begins from between the branches of Sidratul- Muntaha and divides the Araf and the Super Space into eastern and western parts. The Samawaat (this universe) is located in the Eastern Super Space, and the Jannaat is located in the Western Super Space. Thus, from the Samawaat (this universe) one cannot move into the Jannaat directly due to the Barzakh.

―O ye assembly of Jinns and men, if it be ye can pass beyond the zones of the Skies and Lands (this universe), pass ye! Not without authority shall ye be able to pass!‖ [Al Quran 55:33]

The verses under discussion refer to the 'Second Descent': "And certainly, he saw him in the Second Descent, near the Lote Tree (Sidratul-Muntaha)." To understand the Second Descent, one must understand the First Descent. Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) ascended to the Arsh, traveling through the Skies, the Eastern Araf, and Sidratul-Muntaha. The following Hadith confirms this:

―After traveling through the Seven Skies, I was raised to the extreme height. I reached a smooth, flat plain where only the sound of the pen was being heard.‖ [Bukhari]

The Prophet (pbuh) moved to a place where he heard the sound of the Pen. The Pen is located in the Arsh. Thus, Prophet (pbuh) ascended to the Arsh, passing through East Araf and Sidratul-Muntaha. From the Arsh, the Prophet (pbuh) descended into the West Araf through the western branch of Sidratul- Muntaha. Then, from the West Araf, Prophet (pbuh) descended into the Jannaat. This was his First Descent.

The Second Descent

After visiting the Jannaat, the Prophet (pbuh) returned to the Arsh via the same path and then descended into the East Araf, where he met Gabriel again. This was his Second Descent. From the East Araf, he descended into the Samawaat (this universe). Function of Sidratul-Muntaha

In the Araf, Prophet (pbuh) saw many angels being programmed, as the verses under discussion say: ―Covered the Tree (Sidratul-Muntaha) that which cover; sight never swerved, nor did it go wrong! For truly did he see of the signs of his Lord, the Greatest‖ An angel cannot learn on its own—Adam was able to learn the names of the items shown, while the angels could not. The Sidratul-Muntaha infuses knowledge into an angel to enable him to perform his task. It also redesigns the angel for the purpose. Prophet (pbuh) saw thousands of angels were falling on Sidratul-Muntaha, like innumerable insects falling on a tree. The Sidratul-Muntaha tasks and programs the angels according to the instructions of the CCU, and sends them to their job stations. Prophet (pbuh) saw the programming:

―It is narrated on the authority of Anas b. Malik that the Messenger of Allah said: Then I was taken to Sidratul-Muntaha whose leaves were like elephant‘s ears and its fruit like big earthenware vessels. And when it was covered by the Commands (electromagnetic force fields carrying instructions) of Allah, it underwent such a change that none amongst the creation has the power to praise its beauty‖ [Hadith]

―On top of each leaf, there was an angel who covered it with colors which cannot be described. Whenever he covered it by Allah's order (electromagnetic force fields carrying instructions), it would change. One version says: It would turn into sapphire and chrysolite, the beauty of which it is impossible for anyone to praise according to what it merits. On it were moths of gold‖ [Hadith]

According to the above Hadiths, when an angel sits on a leaf, an instrument hangs over him. In the Hadith, this instrument is described as a 'fruit, like earthenware vessel'. When commands (electromagnetic force fields carrying instructions) arrive, they are spurted through the instrument into the angel, and they sparkles with light rays of varying colors. This indicates that the data is infused into the angel through the instrument. In this way, the angel is assigned the knowledge of its task, and its body may be redesigned to fulfill it. The angel is then sent to its assigned station with a group. The CCU is deliberately discussed in Secrion-9 of Chapter-6.

Section 3 of Chapter 53 [Verse 19-30]: None can Intercede

Have ye seen Lat and Uzza and another, the third, Manat? What! For you the male sex, and for Him the female! Behold, such would be indeed a division most unfair! These are nothing but names which ye have devised— you and your fathers—for which God has sent down no authority. They follow nothing but conjecture and what their own souls desire, even though there has already come to them Guidance from their Lord! Or is there for man whatever he wishes? But it is to God that the End and the Beginning belong. How many-so-ever be the angels in the Skies, their intercession will avail nothing, except after God has given leave for whom He pleases, and that he is acceptable to Him. Those who believe not in the Hereafter name the angels with female names. But they have no knowledge therein. They follow nothing but conjecture, and conjecture avails nothing against Truth. Therefore, shun those who turn away from Our Message and desire nothing but the life of this world. That is as far as knowledge will reach them. Verily, thy Lord knows best those who stray from His Path, and He knows best those who receive guidance.

Section 4 of Chapter 53 [Verse 31-55]: Allah is the Sole Authority

Yea, to God belongs all that is in the Skies and Lands so that He rewards those who do evil according to their deeds.

Remarks:

The Skies and the Lands (Samawaat-wal-Ard, or this universe) are full of galaxies. These galaxies will be allotted to the Unbelievers according to their deeds. A great sinner will be given a vast galaxy with trillions of stars. He will be a vicegerent of God over his galaxy, but forgotten. And He rewards those who do good with what is best. Those who avoid great sins and shameful deeds, only small faults—verily thy Lord is ample in forgiveness. He knows you well when He brings you out of the earth and when you are hidden in your mothers' wombs. Therefore, justify not yourselves; He knows best who it is that guards. See thou one who turns back, gives a little, then hardens? What! Has he knowledge of the Unseen so that he can see? Nay, is he not acquainted with what is in the Books of Moses and of Abraham who fulfilled his engagements? Namely: That no bearer of burdens can bear the burden of another, that man can have nothing but what he strives for, that his striving will soon come in sight; then will he be rewarded with a reward complete. That to thy Lord is the Final Goal. That it is He Who grants Laughter and Tears. That it is He Who grants Death and Life. That He created the pairs; the male and the female from a drop when lodged.

Remarks:

Out of 46 chromosomes, two are sex chromosomes:  The sex chromosomes of a male are X and Y chromosomes.  The sex chromosomes of a female are X and X chromosomes. If a zygote forms with the XY chromosome, the baby will be male; and if the zygote forms with the XX chromosome, the baby will be female. In this verse, the point to note is: ―…the male and the female from a drop when lodged.‖ The semen is lodged, and it carries the sperms. The chromosomes of sperms are haploid. So, some sperms carry Y chromosomes, and some carry X chromosomes. If a sperm with Y chromosome fertilizes the egg, the baby becomes male, and vice Versa. Thus, a male or female child is produced due to the sperm, not the ovum. Therefore, men are responsible for the birth of a baby-girl or baby-boy.

The groups of angels initially descend into the Command Station. There are seven Command Stations in seven Skies. The Sirius System

The Sirius is the brightest star visible to the unaided eye. To find Sirius, one may use the Belt of Orion as the pointer—the line of three stars is pointing Sirius to the left. The star is 20 times brighter than the Sun. It is 8.6 (eight-point-six) light years away. FIGURE 53.4: Sirius A and B

In 1862, Alvan Graham Clark discovered that Sirius has a faint companion. It is thought to be a white dwarf. It is named Sirius B. It is 10000 times dimmer than Sirius. Sirius B has a mass 98 percent that of our Sun. Despite its large mass, Sirius B has a diameter of only 12,000 kilometers, making it smaller than Earth and much denser. Its gravitational force is 350,000 times stronger than Earth's gravitational force. The stars in a binary system rotate around a common center of gravity. Some astronomers predict that Sirius may have a third, tiny companion. However, telescopic observations reveal nothing. It could be an opening (portal) to a sub- channel leading from the Command Station of the First Sky, which might resemble a tiny black hole.
