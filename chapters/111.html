Al-Masad / The Plaited Rope

Introduction

The Surah declares the punishment of Abu Lahab, and those who oppose the peaceful preaching of Islam may follow him.

Tafsir of the Surah May the hands of Abu Lahab be ruined and ruined is he! No profit to him from all his wealth and all his gains! Burnt soon will he be in a fire of blazing flame! His wife shall carry the wood as fuel! A twisted rope of palm-leaf-fiber round her neck!

Remarks:

Initially, in Makkah, Prophet (pbuh) preached Islam secretly. He began preaching openly by calling the people of Makkah to gather near a hill, but Abu Lahab threw a stone at him and sabotaged his efforts with abusive words. This incident marked the start of open opposition to his message. During the Prophet's (pbuh) time, people from one tribe would not kill a member of another tribe due to the tradition of revenge killings and the fear of inter-tribal war. The Prophet (pbuh) faced risks not from people of other tribes, but from his own tribe, Banu Hashim. Abu Talib, the Prophet's uncle and the chief of Banu Hashim, was protecting him. But when Abu Talib died, Abu Lahab became the Chief, and the threat on Prophet‘s life became viable and imminent. He soon migrated to Madinah. Abu Lahab was step brother of Abu Talib and Prophet‘s Father, Abdullah. Abu Lahab died after the Battle of Badr, afflicted by malignant pustules, a highly contagious disease. He was abandoned to die alone. When he passed away, his body was disposed of with sticks and buried in a ditch. Thus, after the defeat at Badr, people witnessed how the prophecy was fulfilled: "May the hands of Abu Lahab be ruined and ruined is he! No profit to him from all his wealth and all his gains…"
