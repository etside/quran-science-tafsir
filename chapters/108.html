আল-কাওসার الكوثر

Al-Kawthar / The Abundance

Introduction

The Surah is a call to follow the Quran in order to connect with the Lord.

Tafsir of the Surah To thee have We granted Kauthar. So, connect (salli) to your Lord and sacrifice. Indeed your enemy is he the one cut off.

Remarks:

Traditionally, 'Kauthar' is translated as 'Fountain,' and 'Salli' is translated as 'Prayer.' Thus, the Surah is commonly translated as follows: To thee have We granted the Fount (of Abundance). Therefore to thy Lord turn in Prayer and Sacrifice. For he who hateth thee, he will be cut off. The Surah is often explained as under: A fountain is granted to Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) for the Day of Judgment, when people will be extremely thirsty. Those who harbor hatred for the Prophet (pbuh) will not be allowed to approach the fountain. However, the exact meaning of 'Kawthar' is not known. A fair translator refrains from translating it and keeps it as 'Kawthar.' Some interpret it as the 'Quran,' while others understand it to refer to Hazrat Fatima (R.), who is regarded as the mother of the 12 Imams. And the meaning of 'salli' according to the dictionary is 'connect'. In the above, I have translated this Surah word-for- word. It appears to be a divine call to follow the path outlined by the Quran and the guided Imams in order to connect with the Lord.
