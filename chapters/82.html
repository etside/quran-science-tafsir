Introduction

The surah discusses the Doomsday and the Day of Judgment (Deen), and it touches upon the purpose of life.

Tafsir of the Surah Section 1 of Chapter 82 [Verse 1-5]: Dooms Day

When the Sky is cleft asunder, when the planets (kawkab) are scattered, when the oceans are suffered to burst forth, and when the graves are turned upside down, Each soul shall know what it has sent forward and kept back.

Remarks:

The first (innermost) sky will be cleft asunder by the first blow of the trumpet, causing the fabric of space to shatter. The planets rotate around the Sun due to the design of space-time. If space were to be shattered, the planets would be scattered. Thus, the verses say: ―When the Sky is cleft asunder, when the planets (kawkab) are scattered,…‖ The Earth will fall directly into the Sun, crushing into powder with a single stroke:

―Then, when the first blast is sounded on the Trumpet and the earth is thrown off and its mountains and they are crushed to powder at one stroke‖ [Al Quran 69: 13-14]

The other planets of the solar system will also follow the same fate. The universe will continue to contract for a long time. Eventually, it will collapse into a point. Thus, the universe will become a point of infinite gravitational force, holding all information (photons) and the commands (force fields) of the past. This point can be seen as the Big Crunch. The point will be sustained by the forces, including gravitational force, radiating from His face in form. Thus, it will be sustained in His face of nafs.

―And call not besides God on another god. There is no god but He. Everything will perish except His own face. To Him belongs the command, and to Him will ye be brought back.‖ [Al Quran 28:88]

―All that on it will perish. But will abide the Face of thy Lord, Full of Majesty, Bounty and the Honor.‖ [Al Quran 55: 26–27] The souls (nafs) of living creatures will be brought from Barzakh (Illiyin and Sijjin), but they will have no power in the point of infinite temperature and density (Singularity/Big Crunch). This is the moment when Allah will reprogram the universe to revive it. The universe will be initiated again. It will move into the right hand of His nafs and will attain mass. The revival of the initial universe in a state of heavy mass is referred to in the Quran as Thaqal (Thaqal means 'Heavy Mass'). The Resurrection of the Dead will occur in the Thaqal. Humans will grow like plants grow on the earth, but at an accelerated pace. The evolution of the universe will be temporarily halted at this state for Judgment and Salvation. The nafs (soul) is designed to revive the body in the same sequence in which the body is formed in the mother‘s womb. To resurrect a human, it will require a set of double-helix DNA molecules (23 pairs). The genomic code is a vast program that cannot be imprinted in the nafs. So, a set of DNA molecules will be collected from the remains of each individual. These DNA molecules can survive in nature for several hundred thousand years. A human will be recreated using the DNA molecules collected from his earthly body, ensuring that he cannot deny being the same person when punishment is pronounced. Even his fingerprint will be the same. The verses under discussion suggest that space-time will also play a role in the resurrection, as they state that the graves will be turned upside down: ―And when the graves are turned upside down; each soul shall know what it has sent forward and kept back.‖ The phrase 'graves turned upside down' may refer to the reversal of the strip of space-time that encodes the grave. The grave (a point on the land) preserves a person‘s DNA molecules or a part of them. Therefore, each soul (nafs) will certainly reach the grave. When a nafs is attached to a set of DNA molecules and the necessary support is provided, a human will grow from the point of the grave. Thus, the 'Day of Law' (Yawm-id Deen) will begin when the universe is on the Face of God. He will have the vision of the next universe and will design and program the extremely compressed universe to develop each object, even in the distant future. The present universe (the ongoing cycle) is also designed in this way: ―The initial configuration of the universe may have been chosen by God, or it may itself have been determined by the laws of science. In either case, it would seem that everything in the universe would then be determined by evolution according to the laws of science...‖ –Is Everything Determined by Stephen Hawking. Allah is the Master of the Day of Deen. 'Deen' means 'Law.' He designed and established the natural laws and configured the initial universe to evolve as desired.

―Praise be to God, the Cherisher and Sustainer of the universes; Most Gracious, Most Merciful; Master of the Day of Law (Deen).‖ [Al Quran 1: 2-4]

Similarly, Allah will configure the next universe on the next Day of Deen: ―And indeed, the wicked surely in hellfire. They will burn it. The Day of Deeni (Law)! And they are not absent from it. And, what can make you know what the Day of Deeni (Law) is! Then, what can make you know what the Day of Deeni (Law) is! The Day, not will have power a soul for a soul, anything, and the command that Day with Allah‖ [Al Quran 82: 14-19]

After the Resurrection, the Land of Judgment will be formed with matter taken from the Thaqal, and the living creatures will be moved there.

Section 2 of Chapter 82 [Verse 6-9]: Purpose of Life

O man, what has seduced thee from thy Lord Most Beneficent? Him Who created thee, then fashioned thee, then justified thee in whatever form (of nature) He willed—He rides you. No! But you deny the recompense.

Remarks:

A human is a fragile creature, living in uncertainty. His body and mind long for a connection with Allah, perhaps through an extended elementary soul (ruh) of His nafs. But, many are seduced by the satan jinns with earthly distractions, causing them to forget Allah. Some even worship idols, and become possessed by the jinns.

―A man is like a horse, whose back never remains vacant, either Allah is riding on him, or a satan.‖ [Hadith]

But the Believers are close to Allah, and their relationship with Him can be profound. This is indicated in the following verses:

―Allah is the light of the Skies and Lands. The parable of His light is as if there were a niche and within it a lamp. The lamp enclosed in glass; the glass as if it were a brilliant star, lit from a blessed tree, an olive, neither of the east nor of the west, whose oil is well-nigh luminous, though fire scarce touched it. Light upon light! Allah does set forth parables for men, and Allah knows all things. In houses, which Allah has permitted to be raised to honor for the celebration in them of His name. In them is He glorified in the mornings and in the evenings. They are such men whom neither business nor trade can drive from the remembrance neither of Allah, nor from regular prayer, nor from the practice of regular charity. Their fear is for the day when hearts and eyes will be transformed‖ [Al Quran 24: 35–37] In the above verses, the human body is compared to a glass, and the flame inside the glass is compared to the light of Allah. For an elaborate discussion of this verse, refer to Section-6 of Chapter-18. On Earth, humans are in the process of development. Here, the light of Allah floods the nafs lightly, as a human is not yet capable of bearing full exposure of His light. However, after the resurrection, humans will be fully matured, and on the Day of Judgment, many will shine like the Sun. Though in earthly life, the light of Allah saturates the nafs very lightly, it is necessary because it trains and develops the nafs to sustain His light. It makes a man the perfect home of God. In the above verses, such humans are compared to houses: ―In houses, which Allah has permitted to be raised to honor for the celebration in them of His name. In them is He glorified in the mornings and in the evenings. They are such men whom neither business nor trade can drive from the remembrance neither of Allah, nor from regular prayer, nor from the practice of regular charity. Their fear is for the day when hearts and eyes will be transformed.‖[Al Quran 24:36- 37] The meshing has no limit, as expressed in the following Hadith:

―When I love him, I am his hearing with which he hears, his seeing with which he sees, his hand with which he strikes, and his foot with which he walks.‖ [Hadith-e-Qudsi] When Allah loves any of His servants, He becomes his hearing, seeing, hand, foot, etc. It is not metaphorical hearing, seeing, hand, or foot. The Hadith is clear in this regard: Allah becomes the hand with which he strikes; Allah becomes the foot with which he walks. Thus, Allah becomes his real hand and foot. However, this does not make him God; rather, he becomes a 'Home of God. When one becomes a 'Mumin' by accepting Islam, one is connected to Allah through the mind (qalb) and begins to feel divine love and mercy. While remaining a 'Mumin' and following day-to-day Islam is sufficient for a person to achieve Jannah (paradise), one may strive to advance further by demonstrating true love for Him and by performing additional prayers in accordance with the Qur'an.

Section 3 of Chapter 82 [Verse 10-19]: Day of Judgment

But verily over you are keepers, kind and honorable, writing down. They know all that you do.

Remarks:

There are two angels with every human who record every action of that person. This is the Amal-Nama (Record of Deeds) of the individual, and it will be used as evidence on the Day of Judgment.

As for the Righteous, they will be in bliss; and the Wicked, they will be in the fire, which they will enter on the Day of Deen (Law), and they will not be able to keep away there-from. And what will explain to thee what the Day of Deen is? Again, what will explain to thee what the Day of Deen is? The Day a soul (nafs) will not have power for a soul (nafs) of anything, and command that Day with Allah.

Remarks:

So, the Day of Deen (Law) ends with the Salvation to the Jannaat, and Downfall to the Hell. On that Day, the laws and configuration of the new universe (Samawaat) will be set. Thus, the universe will be organized for the next cycle. It will contain the objects of hell (galaxies).
