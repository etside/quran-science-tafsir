Tafsir of the Surah Section 1 of Chapter 70 [Verse 1-14]: On the Day of Judgment

A questioner asked about a penalty to befall, to the Unbelievers; not of it any preventer from God, Lord of the Ways of Ascent; the angels and the ruh ascend unto him in a Day the measure whereof is fifty thousand years. Therefore, do thou hold patience, a patience beautiful.

Remarks:

The universe will collapse, and subsequently, it will be revived to the state of Thaqal (Heavy Mass), when the resurrection of the dead will occur. The evolution of the universe will be temporarily halted at the state of Thaqal for the Judgment and Salvation. The Land of Judgment will be formed in Super Space, with the matter of the Solar System detached from the Thaqal. Seven channels will connect the Land of Judgment with the Thaqal, and a pair of channels will connect the Land of Judgment with the Araf (see figure below).

The Seven Channels:

―Again, on the Day of Judgment will ye be raised up. And We have made above you Seven Tracts (Channels through the Super space), and We are never unmindful of creation.‖ [Al Quran 23: 16-17]

The Pair of Channels:

Hadith: ―In the Seventh Sky, I saw a river of light (a channel through the Super Space), such that the light that was coming from them made the eyes blind. The river of darkness (a channel through the Super Space) was also there that was covered over with ice, and the sound of thunder crashing could be heard. I was busy looking at these rivers when Jibra'il (Gabriel) said to me, "O' Muhammad, be thankful to Allah for the graces and bounties that have been chosen for you." [Bihar al-Anwar, Vol. 18]

In the verse under discussion, these channels are called Ways of Ascent. The angels who will die during the First Blow of the Trumpet will be resurrected on the Thaqal and will move to the Land of Judgment through the Ways of Ascent (Seven Channels). The jinn and other anti-creatures will be resurrected on the Thaqal and will also be moved to the Land of Judgment through the same Ways of Ascent. However, the resurrected humans and other living creatures made of baryonic matter will be shifted to the Land of Judgment along with the matter of the Solar System, which will form the Land of Judgment. [The Final Judgment is deliberately discuss is Section-6 of Chapter-39]

They see it indeed as a far-off, but We see it near. The Day that the Sky (the Universe at the state of Thaqal) will be like molten brass and the mountains will be like wool. And no friend will ask after a friend, though they will be put in sight of each other. The sinner's desire will be: would that he could redeem himself from the penalty of that Day by his children, his wife, and his brother, his kindred who sheltered him, and all, all that is on earth, so it could save him.

Section 2 of Chapter 70 [Verse 15-35]: Saved Ones

No! Indeed, it is the flame, plucking out right to the skull, inviting such as turn their backs, and turn away their faces, and collect and hide it!

Remarks:

The producers of hell-fire are black holes. The escape velocity of a black hole is so high that even light cannot escape from it. Thus, any object that falls into a black hole is hidden forever. A black hole collects everything from the surrounding space through its immensely powerful gravitational force, resulting in the formation of an accretion disk. The matter swirls around the black hole and gradually collapses toward the central point. This swirling matter produces devastating flames due to friction. Thus, a black hole collects and hides, producing flames due to its nature of gathering and concealing. Similarly, a sinner exhibits the same traits; they collect wealth and hide it, refusing to spend it in charity. Hell calls those of a similar nature: ―…inviting such as turn their backs, and turn away their faces, and collect and hide it!‖

Truly, man was created very impatient—fretful when evil touches him, and niggardly when good reaches him. Not so those who closely follow—those who remain steadfast to their salat. And those in whose wealth are a recognized right for who asks, and for him who is prevented. And those who hold to the truth of the Day of Judgment. And those who fear the displeasure of their Lord; for their Lord's displeasure is the opposite of Peace and Tranquility. And those who guard their chastity except with their wives and whom their right hands possess, for they are not to be blamed, but those who trespass beyond this are transgressors. And those who respect their trusts and covenants (Bayah) And those who stand firm in their testimonies. And those who guard of their salat. Such will be the honored ones in the Jannaat, honored.

Section 3 of Chapter 70 [Verse 36-41]: Unbelievers on the Day of Judgment

Now what is the matter with the Unbelievers that they rush madly before thee from the right and from the left in crowds? Does every man of them aspire to enter the Jannaata-Naimin? No! Indeed, We have created them from that which they know (double helix DNA molecule). So, I swear by the Lord of risings and settings that indeed We are able to replace them with better than them, and We are not to be outdone.

Remarks:

All living creatures, from amoeba to blue whale and bacterium to maple tree, are produced from the same double helix DNA molecule. Only the genetic codes differ. Therefore, it is possible to produce giants suited to live in the objects of hell (galaxies), as the verses under discussion suggest: ―No! Indeed, We have created them from that which they know (double helix DNA molecule). So, I swear by the Lord of risings and settings that indeed We are able to replace them with better than them, and We are not to be outdone.‖ An idolater is a mount for the satanic jinn—he remains possessed, though unaware of it. His soul (nafs) becomes deformed, learning to sustain and inspire both a human and jinn body. His deformed soul is nourished in the Sijjin. He will be resurrected with a giant, devil- human, multi-dimensional body, suited for the object of hell (a galaxy). The people will have the ability to interact with the jinns, created from anti-matter. Their nafses will be so developed that they will be able to observe the jinn with their third-eye vision, alongside the visions of their physical eyes. They will hear the jinns as well. The jinns will be their allies in the galaxy, where they would be the vicegerents of God, but forgotten.

"Such as took their religion to be mere amusement and play, and were deceived by the life of the world." That day shall We forget them as they forgot the meeting of this day of theirs, and as they were wont to reject Our signs." [Al Quran 7:51]

Their physiques will be immensely robust and dynamic. If one's physique is harmed, it will heal spontaneously. Skin and lost body parts will regenerate. A person created with a fixed, matured soul (nafs), programmed to resurrect them from a set of double-helix DNA molecules, cannot be destroyed. The nafs will continuously resurrect them, forever. On the Day of Judgment, a pagan will know his likely destiny by seeing his resurrected body. Yet, some of them will rush madly towards As-Sirat, aspiring to enter the Jannaat.

Section 4 of Chapter 70 [Verse 42-44]: Conclusion

So, leave them to plunge in vain talk and play about until they encounter that Day of theirs, which they have been promised. The Day whereon they will issue from their sepulchers in sudden haste as if they were rushing to a goal post, their eyes lowered in dejection, ignominy covering them! Such is the Day, the which they are promised!
