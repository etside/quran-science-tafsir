Introduction

The Surah acknowledges the right of the Quraysh over the Kaaba, thereby granting them the responsibility of looking after it forever.

Tafsir of the Surah For the covenants by the Quraysh—their covenants journeys by winter and summer. Let them adore the Lord of this House Who provides them with food against hunger and with security against fear.
