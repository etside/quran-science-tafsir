Section 1 of Chapter 55 [Verse 1-4]: Gracious God

Most Gracious! It is He Who has taught the Qur'an. He has created man; He has taught him to talk.

Remarks:

A human is created with a suitable tongue, teeth, lips, nose, throat, lungs, and other structures that enable speech. Innumerable nerves connect the muscles to the highly developed brain to coordinate the actions required for talking. This is not a simple design. We possess fully developed languages. Inspiration may arise from the inherent emotions of the nafs (soul). The brain translates this inspiration into language and generates corresponding commands for the muscles. These commands are transmitted through the nerves, which coordinate the movement of the tongue, teeth, lips, nose, throat, lungs, and other structures, all working together at high speeds to produce words through the mouth. Allah is far greater than that we can think. Section 2 of Chapter 55 [Verse 5-9]: Fall not short in the Balance

The sun and the moon follow courses computed and the herbs and the trees both bow in adoration. And the Sky has He raised, and He has set up the balance, in order that ye may not transgress balance. So, establish weight with justice and fall not short in the balance.

Section 3 of Chapter 55 [Verse 10-25]: The Passing Days

It is He Who has spread out the land for creatures. Therein is fruit, and date-palms producing spathes, also corn with leaves and stalk for fodder, and sweet-smelling plants. Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny? He created man from splendid sauce (salsalin kal fakhari).

Remarks:

In the above verse, 'salsalin kal fakhari' is typically translated as 'sounding clay like unto pottery'. However, 'salsa' actually means 'sauce', and 'fakhari' means 'proud', 'splendid', or similar terms. Therefore, I have translated 'salsalin kal fakhari' as 'splendid sauce'; it can also be translated as 'proud sauce'. Why the sauce is splendid / proud? The basic ingredient in creating a human is amino acids, which can be likened to sauce. A form of it is available in the market. FIGURE 55.1: Amino Acid (‗salsalin kal fakhari‘)

The sauce (amino acid) is described as proud or splendid because of its remarkable qualities. Each amino acid consists of a central carbon, which is bonded to an amine group (NH2), a carboxyl group (COOH), a hydrogen atom, and an R group. The R group varies, leading to the production of 20 different types of amino acids.

FIGURE 55.2: Generic Structure of Amino Acid

The human body is primarily made up of proteins. There are approximately 20,000 types of proteins in the body, all of which are made from the 20 types of amino acids available in a cell. The amino acids are sequenced according to mRNA as a linear chain, which is then folded into specific shapes to form a type of protein. Therefore, the amino acid is a splendid sauce that comes in 20 types and can combine to form 20,000 types of proteins. The sauce (amino acid) deserves to be proud because of its extremely broad spectrum of abilities.

And He created Jinns from the ‗prairie of fire‘ (marijin min narin). Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny?

Remarks:

In the above verses, 'marijin min narin' is commonly translated as 'smokeless flame of fire'. However, 'marijin' actually means 'prairie', 'grassland', or 'lowland'. Therefore, I have translated 'marijin min narin' as 'prairie of fire'. Thus, the jinn are created from something that originates in the prairie of fire. The land should be filled with fire, or it could be a place where anything that falls ignites into flames. What kind of land and what kind of fire are these?

―… and the jinn race We had created before from the poisonous fire‖ [Al Quran 15:27]

So, the prairie of fire produces poisonous fire. All fire burns and destroys; what could poisonous fire be? It seems that the verse is referring to antimatter. When matter and antimatter collide, they annihilate each other, producing light. Therefore, antimatter is poisonous to matter. The jinn exist around us, but we cannot interact with them directly or through instruments. The characteristics of the jinn, as described in the Quran and Hadith, suggest that they may be anti-creatures made of antimatter. Therefore, in the above verse, 'prairie of fire' should be interpreted as 'a deposit of antimatter,' such as a neutron star. When baryonic matter falls onto a neutron star, it produces fire. Therefore, the jinn are anti-creatures made of antimatter. This universe (Samawaat) is a two-in-one universe: one part is visible to us, created from baryonic matter, while the other part is invisible, created from dark matter. This universe contains about six times more dark matter than regular matter. Antimatter is a form of dark matter. This universe is essentially the home of the jinn and an exile for humans, being the children of Adam and Eve.

Lord of the Two Easts and Lord of the Two Wests. Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny?

Remarks:

The Two Easts and Two Wests are often explained as two extreme points on the eastern and western horizons, within which the sun rises and sets over the span of a year. However, I believe the verse is referring to the major entities of the Super Sky (Super Space), with the entire universe located in the Eastern Super Space and the Jannaat situated in the Western Super Space. In addition, the Araf is divided into an Eastern Part and a Western Part. The Eastern Part of the Araf is the primary abode of the angels associated with the Samawaat (this universe), while the Western Part of the Araf is the primary abode of the angels associated with the Jannaat (another universe). These two parts are connected by the two main branches of Sidratul-Muntaha.

FIGURE 55.2: Two Easts and Two Wests

Thus, the Two Easts refer to this universe (Samawaat) and the Eastern Araf, while the Two Wests refer to the Jannaat and the Western Araf.

He has let free the two bodies of flowing water meeting together. Between them is a Barrier, which they do not transgress. Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny? Out of them come Pearls and Coral. Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny?

Remarks:

The corals are living organisms. When they die, they leave behind skeletons that form the rock-like structure. Corals can survive in the warm, saltwater of the oceans. Oysters and mussels, which produce pearls, also require warm water. High-latitude regions (between 60 and 90 degrees latitude in the northern and southern hemispheres) are not suitable for them due to the cold water. Therefore, the above verses are referring to the oceans, excluding the high-latitude regions. Except in high-latitude regions, ocean water is divided by a barrier known as the Pycnocline. The Pycnocline is a layer in the ocean, located between 100 and 1,000 meters below the surface. In this layer, water density increases rapidly with depth due to changes in temperature and salinity. The layer is extremely stable and acts as a barrier, preventing the vertical flow of water and protecting the surface waters (approximately the upper 100 meters). As a result, changes in salinity and temperature are minimal below the Pycnocline but are seasonal in the surface waters. ―Except at high latitudes, the ocean is divided into three horizontal depth zones based on density: the mixed layer, Pycnocline layer, and deep layer‖ – NASA, Ocean Motion. FIGURE 55.3: Layer of the Ocean

The water in the surface layer (Mixed Layer) is mixed with fresh water from rivers and rainfall, making it less saline. This less saline water is ideal for the growth of various types of phytoplankton, which serve as food for zooplankton, the primary food source for fish.

FIGURE 55.4: Mixed Layer The Deep Layer is suitable for decomposers, such as bacteria, protozoa, algae, fungi, etc. Allah, who is capable of creating a barrier (Pycnocline) in the water with the water, is able to maintain both the Universe and the Anti-Universe across different dimensions of space. It should also be noted that the polar and sub-polar regions are not suitable for the production of corals and pearls. Similarly, the polar and sub-polar regions are not conducive to the formation of a Pycnocline. In these regions, the surface water becomes cold and frequently sinks into the deeper layers, disrupting the Pycnocline. The Quran is precise and literally true in every verse.

Segment-2 Tomorrow

Section 4 of Chapter 55 [Verse 26-30]: The Doomsday

Each from it will perish, but will abide the Face of thy Lord, full of Majesty, Bounty and Honor. Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny?

Remarks:

The Skies (Samawaat / this Universe) will be rolled up, ultimately collapsing into a state where matter is annihilated (Big Crunch). Only the information (photons) and forces (commands/ruh) wrapped around a point of extreme gravitational force will survive. The gravitational force is an extended elementary soul of Allah. The super- compact universe will become a bright point on His face of nafs (soul). His face of nafs is formed by the forces, including gravitational force, radiating through His face in form. He will then reprogram the universe to revive it for the next cycle. When He will release the super-compact universe, it will return to the right hand of His nafs, attaining mass (Thaqal), and the resurrection of the dead will occur. The evolution of the reviving universe will be temporarily halted for Judgment and Salvation. The righteous will be taken to another universe called Jannaat, while the sinners will remain in the galaxies of this universe (Samawaat), enduring eternal disasters and pain, as forgotten vicegerents of God.

Of Him seeks every creature in the Skies and Lands; every day He is on! Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny?

Remarks:

Allah is great, and His creations are countless. Reflect on the moment when Allah began the creation of this universe. There were great risks involved. Although Allah first created a computer and devised a plan as a virtual universe, the materialization of that plan was astronomically vast, complex, and risky. He created the universe and has been running it successfully for billions of years. He must complete the cycle, and its completion will make creation cyclic and everlasting. He is continuously working toward this. If He can fulfill His plan, He will appear as a fully successful Creator, Sustainer, Evolver, and Re-Creator. However, a tiny human, thinking, must have faith in Him.

Section 5 of Chapter 55 [Verse 31-32]: The Judgment Day

Soon shall We settle your affairs, O both ye Heavy Masses (Thaqalani)! Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny?

Remarks:

From the Face of God, the universe will be revived to the state of Thaqal (Heavy Mass), where the Resurrection of the dead will take place. During the Final Judgment, two Heavy Masses (Thaqalani) will exist in the Eastern Super Space. One (Main Thaqal) will be formed from all the matter of this universe (Samawaat), excluding the matter of the Solar System. The other, the Land of Final Judgment, will be created from the matter of the Solar System.

Segment-3 The Day after Tomorrow

Section 6 of Chapter 55 [Verse 33-45]: Days in the Hell

O ye assembly of Jinns and men! If it be ye can pass beyond the zones of the Skies and Lands, pass ye! Not without authority shall ye be able to pass! Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny? On you will be sent a flame of fire and a smoke; no defense will ye have. Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny? When the Sky is rent asunder, and it becomes red like ointment. Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny? On that Day no question will be asked of man or jinn as to his sin. Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny? The sinners will be known by their marks, and they will be seized by their forelocks and their feet. Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny? This is the Hell, which the sinners deny. In its midst and in the midst of boiling hot water will they wander round! Then which of the favors of your Lord will ye deny?

Remarks:

After the Judgment, the rewarded individuals will be transferred to the Jannaat (another universe). Nobody can pass beyond the zones of the Skies and Lands (this universe) without authority, as stated in the verses: ―O ye assembly of Jinns and men! If it be ye can pass beyond the zones of the Skies and Lands (this universe), pass ye! Not without authority shall ye be able to pass!‖ The unbelievers will be scattered across the galaxies of this universe; "…they will be seized by their forelocks and their feet." The phrase 'seized by the forelock and feet' refers to being pulled by the gravitational forces of their galaxies. Soon, a person will fall into his destined galaxy. The galaxies of this universe are the objects of Hell, which the sinners deny.
