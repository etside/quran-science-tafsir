IntroductionRemarks:

SCANDALMONGER]

Introduction

The Surah warns scandalmongers and backbiters who are preoccupied with amassing wealth. It describes the Fire of Hell.

Tafsir of the Surah Woe to every scandalmonger and backbiter who piles up wealth and counts it, thinking that his wealth would make him last forever! By no means! He will surely to be thrown into the Crusher, and what will explain to thee what the Crusher is! The Fire of God, kindled, which mounts up to the hearts; verily, it upon them closed over in columns extended.

Remarks:

What could be the ‗Fire of God‘? And what are the Crusher, and the Columns Extended?

1. Fire of God

Everything belongs to Allah, but when something is specifically mentioned as belonging to Him, it indicates a closer relationship to Him. Thus, the Fire of God is a special Fire. The fire is produced by gravitational force, which is a direct force of Allah. This is indicated in the following verses:

―Do they not see towards the birds, controlled in the atmosphere (air) of the sky (Samah)? None holds them except Allah; most surely there are signs in this for a people who believe‖ [Al Quran 16:79]

―Do they not observe the birds above them, spreading and folding? None can uphold them except (God) Most Gracious: Truly it is He that watches over all things.‖ [Al Quran 67:19]

In the above verses, Allah gives the example of flying birds. A bird flies by spreading and folding its wings, but it would be unbalanced and thrown off course if not for gravity holding it through its center of gravity (CG). The holding of birds by gravity is described in the above verses as a direct act of Allah.

―He covers the night with the day, seeking it rapidly, and the sun and the moon and the stars controlled by His deed‖ [Al Quran 7:54]

Therefore, gravitational force is a force of Allah. A force field within a living entity can be called the soul. Thus, the gravitational force field can be considered an elementary soul (ruh) of Allah. Allah is deliberately discussed in Chapter 1. The nafs (composite soul) of Allah, which permeates His 'body in shape,' is a combination of many known and unknown force fields (ruhs/elementary souls), along with possibly other elements unknown. He has extended 15 to 20 of these force fields beyond His body in shape, creating His 'hands of nafs.' Allah has designed the forces of His hands of nafs to act in fixed patterns according to His will-power and has infused these into the universes to sustain and evolve creation. We observe the effects of these forces as several of natural laws. The whole universe is in the palm of His right hand (right hand of nafs). The hand comprises 15 to 20 forces. The gravitational force is one of these forces. It is a force of Allah. He is the Sustainer of the universes. How gravitational force produces fire? The gravitational force exerted by a black hole is so intense that even light cannot escape from its surface. With its immensely powerful gravitational pull, a black hole draws in materials from the surrounding space. As these materials approach the singularity, they spin faster and faster, generating significant friction. This friction produces heat, and eventually, the temperature becomes so extreme that it releases massive amounts of radiation, including x-rays, gamma rays, visible light, and radio waves. The galaxies are objects of hell. Every galaxy harbors a super-massive black hole at its center, where gravitational force converts massive amounts of matter into energy. Since gravitational force is a force of God, the verses under discussion refer to these gigantic emissions as the 'Fire of God': ―The Fire of God, kindled, which mount up to the hearts‖ 2. Crusher

A black hole can quickly absorb small amounts of matter without releasing much energy. If a small stone is thrown into a black hole, it will simply vanish, being compressed to a size smaller than dust. However, if the amount of matter is large, the black hole cannot absorb it immediately. ―But if the mass of several million stars is compressed around it, there will be a massive pile up of material- gas, dust and even whole stars- sucked in by the intense gravity field but unable to squeeze immediately into the tight ‗throat‘ funneling down into the hole‖ – The Life and Death of Stars by Geoffrey Bath in The Encyclopedia of Space Travel and Astronomy edited by John Man As the materials collapse, the black hole spins at tremendous speed, winding its strong magnetic field around itself. Due to the rotating magnetic field, the collapsing material cannot fall into the black hole immediately. Instead, it spreads into a blanket of in- falling matter in the plane of rotation, similar to Saturn's rings but on a much larger scale. This is called the Accretion Disk, where swirling materials are crushed, and energy is released due to friction. The verses under discussion refer to the Accretion Disk as the 'Crusher': ―By no means! He will surely to be thrown into the Crusher, and what will explain to thee what the Crusher is!‖ 3.
