Section 1 of Chapter 91 [Verse 1-10]: Nafs (Soul)

By the sun and his splendor—by the moon as she follows him. By the day as it shows up glory—by the night as it conceals it. By the sky and Who constructed it—and the lands, and by Who spread it. By the nafs (soul) and Who proportioned it—and He inspired it its wickedness and its righteousness. Truly, he succeeds that purifies it, and he fails that buries it!

Remarks:

The nafs is one of the two souls a human being. It is the basic soul. A human nafs is a combination of unknown force fields (elementary souls/ruh). These force fields are the sources of emotions and primarily influence the body through the mind and brain. For example, anger may be visible on one's face, but the emotion is rooted in a force field of the nafs. A person without a nafs would resemble an emotionless robot, like an artificial intelligence. Another soul is a special ruh (elementary soul / unknown force field) that is directly given by God after a human's birth. It provides the field of his mind (Qalb / virtual brain). [The nafs and the ruh are deliberately discussed in Section-10 of Chapter-6]. Allah has proportioned the nafs with different types of force fields in such a way that it maintains balanced nature of the human. However, he can rise in either wickedness or righteousness. The verse under discussion also applies to the soul of an atom. Three force fields operate within an atom: the Magnetic Force Field, the Strong Nuclear Force Field, and the Weak Nuclear Force Field. These three force fields (ruhs), in combination, form the nafs (soul) of the atom. Each of these force fields is a ruh (elementary soul) and performs specific functions. The force fields of a nafs can interact with antimatter, just as gravitational force can act on both matter and antimatter. Therefore, a human nafs can influence the jinn, who are created from antimatter. The soul (nafs) of an atom weakens if it loses energy. When it loses energy, it sheds its outer shells, causing the soul to be diminished. Similarly, a human soul (nafs) is energized by the light of Allah. When the light of Allah diminishes, the soul weakens. The soul of an atom is abruptly weakened if it is struck by antiparticles. Similarly, a human soul (nafs) is abruptly buried if a jinni, created from antimatter, possesses it. The extent of burial correlates with the level of possession. It should be noted that a jinni, created from antimatter, cannot directly possess a human. Instead, it does so through its own nafs and the human's nafs. A jinni can only possess a pagan. It cannot possess a believer, as believers are protected by angels. However, it can whisper evil thoughts to them. The pulses of the brain also illuminate the nafs. The brain assesses situations and sends electrical impulses throughout the body to stir up emotions. In turn, the emotions that arise drive the human through the brain. A brain having correct faith and knowledge will assess the situations accurately, which will arouse the right emotions. Ultimately, the right emotions will drive the human on the right path.

How to remain clean by driving away the satan jinn?

A satanic jinni possesses a human to partake in the pleasures of human life. Therefore, renounce everything that brings joy: luxurious homes, cars, friends, food, music, movies, travel, and everything else that provides pleasure. A human has eyes for color vision, ears with a broad spectrum of hearing, and skin that senses comfort. Therefore, close your eyes and ears, turn off the AC, avoid sitting on a couch, and refrain from lying on a soft bed. A human also possesses the power of imagination, which can transport him into virtual (mental) enjoyment. Therefore, cease thinking. If it feels too difficult, then surrender completely to Allah and follow the Quran strictly. Is it still too difficult? Then, at the very least, maintain your faith (Iman) in one God and the Last Day, and do good deeds. A satanic jinni is not allowed to possess a believer of one God; it can only whisper to them. [The Jinn is deliberately discussed in Section 3 of Chapter 7]

Section 2 of Chapter 91 [Verse 11-15]: Thamud were Obliterated

The Thamud rejected through their inordinate wrongdoing. Behold, the most wicked man among them was deputed. But the apostle of God said to them: "It is a she-camel of God and having her drink!" Then they rejected him, and they hamstrung her. So, their Lord on account of their crime obliterated their traces and made them leveled! And not He fears its consequences.
