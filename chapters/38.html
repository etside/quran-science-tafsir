Tafsir of the Surah Section 1 of Chapter 38 [Verse1-3]: The Quran is full of Reminders

Sad, by the Qur'an, full of Reminder. Nay, those who disbelieve in self-glory and opposition—how many generations before them did We destroy! In the end, they cried when there was no longer time for being saved! Section 2 of Chapter 38 [Verse 4-15]: People rejecting Prophet (pbuh)

So, they wonder that a Warner has come to them from among themselves! And the Unbelievers say: ―This is a sorcerer telling lies. Has he made the gods into one God! Truly, this is a wonderful thing.‖ And the leader among them goes away: "Walk ye away and remain constant to your gods; for this is truly a thing designed. We never heard of this among the people of these latter days; this is nothing but a made-up tale! What! Has the Message been sent to him among us!" But they are in doubt concerning My message! Nay, they have not yet tasted My punishment! Or have they the treasures of the mercy of thy Lord, the Exalted in Power, the Grantor of Bounties without measure? Or have they the dominion of the Skies and the Lands and all between? If so, let them mount up with the ropes and means! But they will be a defeated host like the confederates of the old times. Before them rejected apostles: the people of Noah, and 'Ad, and Pharaoh the Lord of Stakes, and Thamud, and the people of Lut, and the Companions of the Wood; such were the Confederates. Not one but rejected the apostles but My punishment came justly and inevitably. These only wait for a single mighty blast, which will brook no delay.

Section 3 of Chapter 38 [Verse 16-29]: Our Servant David

They say: "Our Lord! Hasten for us our share before the Day of Account!" Have patience at what they say and remember Our servant David, the man of strength, for he ever turned. It was We that made the hills declare in unison with him Our praises at eventide and at break of day, and the birds gathered; all with him did turn.

Remarks:

In above verses, Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) is said, ―Have patience at what they say and remember Our servant David‖. What is there to remember about David? He was not responsible for preaching religion among unbelievers. He was the son-in-law of a king, later became a king himself, and died in old age as a king. He faced nothing like Prophet Muhammad (pbuh). Yet, David is to be remembered for his turning to God, as said in the verses: ―…remember Our servant David, the man of strength, for he ever turned. It was We that made the hills declare in unison with him Our praises at eventide and at break of day, and the birds gathered; all with him did turn.‖ It is said in another verse as well:

―We bestowed grace afore-time on David from ourselves: "O ye mountains sing ye back the praises of God with him, and ye birds...‖‖ [Al Quran 34:10]

The remembrance (zikr) of God calms the heart. One should remember and praise God in the moments of sorrow and joy. The verses narrate the effects of praising God. To understand the effects, we should ask some question:  How did the hills praise God? They do not have mouths—were they merely echoing the sound, or were they praising in a way similar to the trees? Trees praise God, yet we do not hear them. Was the praising of the hills audible to the human ear?  Were the birds praising in David‘s language, or were they praying in their own languages? Most likely, the verses imply that if a person praises God loudly and regularly in a specific area, maybe the nearby objects and living creatures is also praising with him, even though the person cannot hear it. For instance, if a person is loudly praising God (zikr) in his house, perhaps the furniture in the house is also praising along with them. The double-slit experiment suggests that human consciousness can affect subatomic particles, such as electrons. An electron moves in the form of a wave, but when it is observed, it behaves like a particle. This implies that an electron may possess some form of consciousness. Therefore, a nafs, enhanced through praising and meditation on God, may establish a limited connection with the local environment and may potentially have an impact on the nearby objects. It is beneficial to set aside a fixed time and space to praise God loudly with beautiful words in a rhythmic tone (preferably selecting words from the Quran). The Quran repeatedly encourages glorifying God at night.

We strengthened his kingdom and gave him wisdom and sound judgment in speech and decision. Has the story of the disputants reached thee? Behold, they climbed over the wall of the private chamber when they entered the presence of David, and he was terrified of them. They said: ―Fear not, we are two disputants, one of whom has wronged the other—decide now between us with truth, and treat us not with injustice, but guide us to the even path. This man is my brother. He has nine and ninety ewes, and I have one; yet he says, ―Commit her to my care, and is harsh to me in speech.‖ Said: "He has undoubtedly wronged thee in demanding thy ewe to be added to his ewes. Truly, many are the partners who wrong each other; not so do those who believe and work deeds of righteousness, and how few they are!" And David gathered that We had tried him. He asked forgiveness of his Lord, fell down bowing, and turned. So, We forgave him this. He enjoyed, indeed, a near approach to Us, and a beautiful place of return.

Remarks:

David had an affair with a married woman. Later, he repented and sought forgiveness from God, and God forgave him. Humans are slaves of God, and a Master has the authority to forgive any wrongdoing committed against any of His slaves. According to the verses, there were two reasons why Allah not only forgave David but also allowed him to maintain his close relationship with Him and ensured his beautiful place of return in the afterlife. The reasons were:  Firstly, David used to praise God in the morning and in the eventide.  Secondly, he ever turned to God by repenting and seeking forgiveness for his sins. It is likely that David praised God with songs and musical instruments. Composing a song of praise with the right words requires exceptional intelligence. Moreover, singing praises to God demands an overflowing love for Him; otherwise, it risks becoming a mockery or mere showmanship. The story of the woman is deliberately narrated in the Holy Bible. However, the account appears to be significantly corrupted. Below is the narration from the Holy Bible, followed by a discussion of the likely corruptions and their possible causes.

Section 4 of Chapter 38 [Verse 30-40]: Solomon

To David We gave Solomon. How excellent in Our service! Ever did he turn! When there were displayed before him in the evening well-trained horses of highest breed, he said, "Truly do I love the ‗love of good‘ with a view to the glory of my Lord," until (the sun) was hidden in the veil: "Bring them back to me." Then began he to pass his hand over legs and their necks. Remarks:

In the above verses, "then began he to pass his hand over their legs and necks" means that he began to kill the horses by cutting off their necks and legs. Solomon is renowned for his sound judgment. If he missed the prayer by visiting them, he himself should have been punished, why the horses were killed? However, the Quran also does not present the incident negatively. Why? The following may offer an explanation: His songs reveal that, personally, he was a man of Sufi ideology, and therefore, he was not supposed to be attached to earthly possessions. However, he felt a deep love for the horses. In response, he was consoling his mind, as the verses say: "Truly do I love the ‗love of good‘ with a view to the glory of my Lord". It is true that the horses could be used to glorify God by strengthening his army, which fought for the cause of God. However, when he missed his prayer, he realized that he had been deceiving himself. He understood that his 'love for the horses' had diverted him from the 'love of God.' In his realization, he began killing the horses as if in a frenzy. A core idea in Sufi ideology is that a person who claims to 'Love God' must direct his love away from everything else.
