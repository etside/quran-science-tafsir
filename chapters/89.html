আল-ফাজর الفجر

Al-Fajr / The Dawn

Introduction

The Surah speaks about people who are in conflict with one another for power and wealth. It calls on the simple souls to return.

Tafsir of the Surah Section 1 of Chapter 89 [Verse1-5]: Profitable times for Prayer

By the break of day, by the nights twice five, by the even and odd, and by the night when it passes away—is there in these an adjuration for those who understand?

Section 2 of Chapter 89 [Verse 6-14]: When People are given More

See thou not how thy Lord dealt with the 'Ad of the Iram with lofty pillars, the like of which were not produced in the land, and with the Thamud who cut out rocks in the valley, and with Pharaoh, lord of stakes? These transgressed beyond bounds in the lands and heaped therein mischief. Therefore, did thy Lord pour on them a scourge of diverse chastisements; for thy Lord is on a watch-tower.

Section 3 of Chapter 89 [Verse 15-20]: When People are given Less

Now, as for man, when his Lord tries him giving him honor and gifts then he says, "My Lord has honored me." But when He tries him restricting his subsistence for him then he says, "My Lord has humiliated me!" Nay, nay! But ye honor not the orphans! Nor do ye encourage one another to feed the poor! And ye devour inheritance, all, with greed. And ye love wealth with inordinate love!

Section 4 of Chapter 89 [Verse 21-26]: Sorrow in the End

Nay! When the Land is pounded to powder and thy Lord comes, and His angels, rank upon rank; and Hell that Day is brought—on that Day will man remember, but how will that remembrance profit him?

Remarks:

After the universe begins to revive from the Big Crunch and gain mass (Thaqal), Israfil will be resurrected and will immediately resume blowing the Trumpet, as he died while blowing it (Part-2 of the First Blow). All resurrected creatures will lose their senses. Due to the extreme sound of the Trumpet, the solar matter ejected from the Thaqal (Heavy Mass) will be reduced to powder. The powdered matter will then come together to form the Land of Judgment. The Land of Judgment will be flat and smooth; it will appear as a disc floating in Super Space.

He will say: "Ah! Would that I had sent forth for my Life!" For that Day His chastisement will be such as none can inflict. And His bonds will be such as none can bind.

Section 5 of Chapter 89 [Verse 27-30]: Successful are those that are satisfied with God

O nafs (soul) in rest and satisfaction! Come back thou to thy Lord well pleased, and well pleasing unto Him! Enter thou then among My devotees! Yea, enter thou My Jannaat!
