How much the earth takes away? The entire genetic makeup of an animal is encoded in a set of its DNA double helix molecules—this forms the blueprint of its life. A team of researchers claims to have discovered genetic material from prehistoric bacteria that is 419 million years old. An earlier sample is believed to be 250 million years old. The oldest Neanderthal DNA, found in a Belgian cave, is estimated to be 100,000 years old. Scientists calculate that under ideal preservation conditions, DNA bonds would begin to break down after 6.8 million years. Additionally, they require long stretches of DNA to replicate it. Therefore, it is not possible for them to recreate dinosaurs that lived 65 million years ago. But is it possible for God to recreate dinosaurs from their DNA? The Earth is both a good place to dwell and an excellent storage. In the following verse, Allah says that an individual will be recreated with the same fingerprint, which is unique to each person. Yes, We are able to put together in perfect order the very tips of his fingers.‖ [Al Qur'an 75:4]

Section 2 of Chapter 50 [Verse 5-14]: In a State of Confusion

But they deny the Truth, when it comes to them, so they are in a confused state. Do they not look at the sky above them? How We have made it, and adorned it, and there is no hole (furujin) in it?

Remarks:

In the above verse, "the sky above them" refers to the sky close to the Earth, which protects us through the magnetosphere and atmosphere. There are no holes in these spheres, as the verses above state: ―…there is no hole (furujin) in it‖. However, there are holes in the ozone layer. But the holes are over the Poles, not above the area where humans live. The ozone layer is found mainly in the lower stratosphere, from 15 to 35 kilometers above the Earth. The layer contains high concentration of ozone (O3). It absorbs harmful ultraviolet radiation of the Sun. The life could not survive on the land without the ozone layer.

FIGURE 1.1: Hole of Ozone Layer

The ozone hole is not a 'hole' where no ozone is present, but rather a region of exceptionally depleted ozone in the Southern Hemisphere, occurring between August and October. No human lives at the South Pole (in Antarctica), so the hole does not constitute a part of the "sky above them". The Northern Hemisphere does not have a large, recurring ozone hole like the Southern Hemisphere. There are two holes in the magnetosphere through which small amounts of harmful solar wind (streams of photons and electrons) enter the Earth. However, the charged particles collide with the atmosphere and are neutralized. These holes in the magnetosphere are also located above the Polar Regions.

And the land, We have spread it out, and set thereon mountains standing firm, and grown therein every kind from ―Pairs Attractive‖ (Zawgin-Baheej)—to be observed and commemorated by every devotee turning. And We send down from the sky rain charged with blessing, and We produce therewith gardens and grain for harvests and tall palm trees with shoots of fruit stalks piled one over another as sustenance for servants, and We give life therewith to land that is dead—so will be the Resurrection.

Remarks:

Allah has designed the land through continental drift and set the mountains in place. The land is full of life. All life forms are created from double helix DNA molecules. In the verses above, the 'DNA molecules' are referred to as 'Attractive Pairs'. A single round of DNA replication precedes cell division. DNA replication is the process of producing two identical replicas from one original double helix DNA molecule. This biological process occurs in all living organisms and is the basis of biological inheritance. A double helix DNA molecule unwinds and divides during replication. Each strand acts as a template for the new strand. Bases are paired to synthesize the new complementary strands. The old strands attract the new strands, forming the new double helix DNA molecule. FIGURE 50.2: DNA Replication

A strand attracts nucleotides from the cytoplasm in the correct sequence to produce a new strand. The new strand is connected to the old strand by weak hydrogen bonds, so the attraction is similar to a magnetic pull. These strands can segregate and integrate as needed. Therefore, in the verses under discussion, they are referred to as 'Attractive Pairs' (zawgin-baheej). A double helix DNA molecule releases different types of mRNA (messenger RNA) to produce various proteins in the ribosome, with the help of rRNA (ribosomal RNA) and tRNA (transfer RNA). The tRNA collects the correct amino acids (out of 20 types) from the cytoplasm. These components are also perfectly attractive, indeed. FIGUTE 50.3: Protein Synthesis

He sends rain, and the rain brings the dead ground to life by producing new life. The 'Attractive Pairs' (double helix DNA molecules) are the foundation for making the Earth alive. A seed remains dormant, but as soon as the rain comes, the codes in the DNA double helix bring it to life. DNA replication begins, dividing the cells, and the plant germinates. Likewise, the Resurrection of mankind will occur, as stated in the verses above: ―…and We give life therewith to land that is dead—so will be the Resurrection.‖ So will be the Resurrection:

A tiny embryonic plant, carrying a small food reserve in its seed coat, can germinate with just a little rainfall. Why, then, shouldn't humans grow from the Earth when Allah has put the necessary supports in place? The double helix DNA molecules of each human are preserved in nature, and survive for millions of years. And each human has his programmed nafs and memory data. A little help may be needed to recreate him in full form. The Resurrection is deliberately discussed in Section- 6 of Chapter-39.
