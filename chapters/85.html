Introduction

The Surah urges Muslims to remain steadfast in their faith and declares severe punishment for those who persecute them.

Tafsir of the Surah Section 1 of Chapter 85 [Verse 1-9]: Fortress

By the sky, the Fortresses. By the Promised Day, and witness, and what is witnessed.

Remarks:

Angels descend to Earth to monitor events related to living creatures. They do not directly visit workplaces; instead, they come through special objects.

―Allah is He Who created Seven Skies and the lands an equivalent. Through the midst of them descends His command that ye may know that Allah has power over all things, and that comprehends all things in knowledge.‖ [Al Quran 65:12]

According to the above verse, the Commands of Allah are descended through seven special lands, positioned in Seven Skies. I call these lands (astral objects) ―Command Stations‖. There is one Command Station in each Sky. In the Quran, a Command Station is referred to as a 'Lofty Station':

―Also mention in the Book the case of Idris: He was a man of truth, a prophet: And We raised him to a Lofty Station‖ [Al Quran 19:56-57]

During the Miraj (Night Journey), the Prophet (pbuh) visited different prophets in the Lofty Stations of the Skies. A Fortress is a star or star-like object associated with a Command Station / Lofty Stations, where the descending angels are temporarily accommodated. There are many Fortresses under each Command Station.

―It is We who have set out Fortresses in the Skies and made them fair-seeming to beholders, and We have guarded them from every satans accursed. But any that gains a hearing by stealth, is pursued by a flaming fire, bright‖ [Al Quran 15: 16–18]

In the Fortresses, the angels wait for their turn to carry out their duties. Their forms vary according to the nature of their tasks. Thus, by observing the angels, the near future can be discerned. The satanic jinns attempt to learn the future by watching and listening to the angels residing in the Fortresses. Angels are created from light, which is why they are stationed in stars, where they can remain energetic. Sirius is one such star.

That He is the Lord of Sirius (the brightest visible star in the night sky). And that it is He Who destroyed the ancient 'Ad‘. And Thamud, nor gave them a lease of perpetual life. And before them the people of Noah, for that they were most unjust and most insolent transgressors. And He destroyed the overthrown cities. [Al Quran 53: 49–53]

The above verses discuss the annihilated nations after mentioning that Allah is the Lord of Sirius. It is likely that Sirius is a Fortress harboring the angels of destruction. After completing their missions, the angels return to the Retiring Fortresses as witnesses. The Prophets and Al-Muttaqin are also considered witnesses.

Woe to the makers of the pit! Fire supplied with fuel, when they were sitting near it, and they witnessed that they were doing against the Believers. Remarks:

The incident took place in Southern Arabia around 523 CE. Dhu Nuwas, the Jewish king of Yemen, captured Najran, which was inhabited by many Christians. He attempted to eliminate Christianity by forcing its followers to convert to Judaism. When the Christians refused, a large number of them were burned in a ditch of fire.

And they ill-treated them for no other reason than that they believed in God, Exalted in Power, Worthy of all Praise! Him, to Whom belongs the dominion of the Skies and Lands! And God is Witness to all things.

Section 2 of Chapter 85 [Verse 10-16]: Persecuting Believers

Those, who persecute the Believers, men and women, and do not turn in repentance will have the penalty of hell; they will have the penalty of the burning fire. For those who believe and do righteous deeds will be Jannaat, beneath which rivers flow; that is the great salvation. Truly, strong is the grip of thy Lord. It is He Who creates from the very beginning, and He can restore. And He is the Oft-Forgiving, Full of Loving-Kindness, Owner of the Glorious Arsh, Doer of all that He intends. Section 3 of Chapter 85 [Verse 17-22]: Conclusion

Has the story reached thee of the Forces of Pharaoh and the Thamud? And yet the Unbelievers are rejecting! But God does encompass them from behind! Nay, this is a Glorious Qur'an in Lawh-Mahfuz!
