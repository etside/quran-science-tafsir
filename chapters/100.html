Al-'Adiyat / Those That Run

Introduction

The Surah points out the expectation from a Muslim

Tafsir of the Surah By that run panting, and strike sparks of fire, And push home the charge in the morning, And raise the dust in clouds the while, And penetrate forthwith into the midst en masse.

Remarks:

' "By that run painting" is typically translated as "by the horse that runs in the painting". While this interpretation is commonly accepted, there is no word in the verse that directly means 'horse.'. The verses actually paint the vivid picture of a soldier charging in a conventional 'Dawn Attack.' A Dawn Attack is an assault where the enemy is engaged in reduced visibility, and reorganization or pursuit is done once daylight breaks. In modern times, penetration by waves of foot soldiers is one of the most dangerous forms of operation. In this strategy, an advancing military formation, supported by air, armor, and artillery, pushes through a series of fortified enemy defenses to capture a vital objective deep within enemy territory. In the process of penetration, each enemy-held locality is captured through a deliberate attack. The attacking foot soldiers move out from a Forming-Up Place (FUP) to seize the enemy's defensive position on a 'do or die' mission. The above verses depict a charging soldier. His objective may be half a kilometer ahead, but this distance is under intense enemy surveillance and coordinated fire from fortified trenches. Although he has fire support, but how much can it help against opponents entrenched for days? He wears a helmet and life jacket, but how much protection do they offer against high-velocity spinning bullets? In reality, many soldiers leave these burdens behind in the FUP, knowing that the first few waves of attack are unlikely to survive, and agility is more important in a running fight. This method of operation was developed by the Germans in the 20th century and is still considered a fundamental military tactic. But isn‘t it surprising that such a battle is depicted in a 1,400-year-old Surah? Read the above verses again; if you have received any higher military training, it will become clear that the verses vividly portray a foot soldier charging in a Dawn Attack.

Truly, man is to his Lord ungrateful, and to that he bears witness. And violent is he in his love of wealth. Does he not know, when that which is in the graves is scattered abroad, and that which is in the breasts is made manifest—that their Lord had been well acquainted with them…that Day…! Remarks:

A dedicated soldier sacrifices his life for the causes of nation or ideology. But what have we done for our Creator? How much have we sacrificed? Violent we are in our love for wealth! On the Day of Judgment, each person will realize that his Lord was fully aware of them. He provided him with land to live on, gave him food and shelter, and healed him from diseases. Yet, he was unaware of Him—he was loyal to something else!
