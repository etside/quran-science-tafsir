Introduction

This Surah simply explains the concept of one God and presents compelling arguments in this regard.

Tafsir of the Surah Say: He is God, the One and Only; God, the Eternal, Absolute. He begets not, nor is He begotten. And there is none like unto Him.

Remarks:

Allah is One and Only. If Allah had a child, He would no longer be One; He would become two. Therefore, He does not have a child. He is Eternal. He did not take birth, nor does He give birth. There is nothing like unto Him. Everything around us is created. So, people often ask, ―Who created God?‖ Actually, nothing is created; everything is transformed into its present form. Absolute creation is not possible for Allah either. He provided (breathed out) a part of His Soul (Nafsin-Wahidatin / a Single Soul / GUT Force+) from His own Nafs and transformed it into creation. Absolute annihilation is also not possible.
