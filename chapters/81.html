Tafsir of the Surah Section 1 of Chapter 81 [Verse 1-6]: The First Blow of the Trumpet

When the sun is folded up. When the stars fall losing their luster. When the mountains vanish. When the she-camels, ten months young, are left untended. When the wild beasts are herded together. When the oceans boil over with a swell.

Remarks:

We must consider the following realities in order to discuss the verses:  This universe (Samawaat) was created in the previous cycle from a part of the Nafsin-Wahidatin (a Soul Single / a nafs / a composite force field / GUT Force+). A part of Nafsin-Wahidatin disintegrated into three force fields (elementary souls / ruhs), namely the Strong Nuclear Force Field, the Electromagnetic Force Field, and Weak Nuclear Force Field. These force fields primarily produced hydrogen and helium, resulting in what is commonly known as the Big Bang. For us, it was Big Bang-1. [It is discussed in Section 4 of Chapter 21.]  When Allah infused (istawa) gravitational force into the smoke, the universe contracted, leading to the production of heavier elements at least up to silicon, which formed dust clouds and asteroids. [This is discussed in Section-4 of Chapter-21.]  The contracting single-sky-universe of the preceding cycle underwent a Big Bounce and became organized into seven-sky-universe of the present cycle. These skies are waves of space, one nested within another, like the layers of an onion. We reside in the First (Innermost) Sky, while the Seventh Sky is the outermost. [The Skies are discussed in Section-7 of Chapter-2].  The Big Bang-1 was spinning, meaning it had an axis. Alternatively, when the universe of the preceding cycle (First Cycle) began contracting, it started spinning and developed an axis. This spinning continued throughout the contraction and the Big Bounce. As a result, the current seven-sky universe is spinning and it has an axis. [This is discussed in Section-7 of Chapter-30].  The rate of spin decreased as the current seven-sky universe expanded in diameter. However, for the past five billion years, the universe has been contracting in diameter, and the rate of spin has been increasing from the Seventh Sky. [This is discussed in Section-7 of Chapter-30].  The drive of contraction has reached the Third Sky. Soon, the Second Sky will be affected, and the First (innermost) Sky will begin to disengage from the overall universe. The First Sky will then start contracting directly toward the center, defying the general 'rolling contraction pattern' of the universe. As a result, the First Sky will enter an 'Independent Contraction Phase' that will last for about 40 years. [This is discussed in Section-7 of Chapter-30].

During the Independent Contraction Phase of the First Sky, the following will occur, in light of the Hadith:  The Sun will rise from the west, as the Cosmological Arrow of Time will reverse in the First Sky due to its direct contraction.  Babies will stop growing in their mother's womb, and people will begin to grow younger, as the Thermodynamic Arrow of Time will reverse.  Everything, from sticks to shoelaces, will express the secrets (future). People will begin to remember the future instead of the past, as the Psychological Arrow of Time will reverse.  The sound of the Trumpet will emanate from the center (the Great Attractor) of the First Sky, and it will take 40 years to reach the Earth. At that point, people will die, and the First Sky will disintegrate, causing its objects to rejoin the overall contraction of the universe. Israfil will die, and eventually, the universe will collapse into a singular point.

Now, let us discuss the verses under consideration: The verses describe the effects of the first blow in reverse order—beginning with the last event and then moving backward through the preceding events. Indeed, they narrate events of reversed time. Therefore, I will approach the verses from the bottom up, starting with the last verse: ―When the oceans boil over with a swell‖

Step 1:―When the oceans boil over with a swell‖

The trumpet will produce multiple frequencies, as it is designed to destroy everything. It has many mouths through which the sounds will be emitted. Initially, the audible sound will not reach Earth, but electromagnetic waves, including those up to the microwave level, will. As a result, the water in the oceans will heat up and expand, surging toward the coastal regions in a manner resembling a tsunami. This is mentioned in the verse: ―When the oceans boil over with a swell‖ We observe a similar effect in a microwave oven. Our food contains water molecules, which have north and south poles, causing them to align with the electromagnetic field. As the electromagnetic wave changes, the water molecules rock back and forth rapidly, and molecular friction generates heat. The frequencies emitted by the Trumpet may produce a similar effect in the oceans. It will kill the sea creatures by overheating the water, but humans and other land animals will not perish. This is the first visible effect of the blow.

Step 2: ―When the wild beasts are herded together‖

The blow will produce sound in the air. Initially, the sound will be below the human hearing range, but many other animals will hear it. They will be scared and will seek refuge in human habitations, as mentioned in this verse: ―When the wild beasts are herded together‖.

Step 3: ―When the she-camels, ten months young, are left untended‖

Gradually, the sound will increase, and humans will begin to hear it. They will be terrified and realize that the Day of Doom has arrived. The mental state of a person during that time is described in this verse: ―When the she- camels, ten months young, are left untended‖.

Step 4:―When the mountains vanish;‖

According to the Hadith, initially the intensity of the sound will be low, but it will gradually increase. Eventually, it will become so loud that people's eardrums will burst, and all living creatures will die. The increasing sound will cause sustained vibrations in everything, and the mountains will melt into dust. This is mentioned in this verse: ―When the mountains vanish;‖ The same is mentioned in the following verse as well: ―One day the earth and the mountains will be in violent commotion. And the mountains will be as a heap of sand poured out and flowing down.‖ [Al Quran 73:14]

Step 5:―When the stars fall, losing their luster;‖

The stars will fall into the central super-massive black holes of their respective galaxies. This is mentioned in the verse: ―When the stars fall, losing their luster;‖ The sound of the first blow will emanate from the Great Attractor, which will be weakened. As a result, the galaxies of the First (innermost) Sky will scatter and rejoin the outer Skies, closing rapidly at that time. Eventually, all the objects in the Universe will converge into one point.

Step 6:―When the sun is folded up;‖

The Skies (the Universe) will be compressed to the point where matter will disappear; only the forces (commands) and information (photons) will survive in a point of singularity. Allah will reprogram the universe and release it for revival. As it will gain mass, the Resurrection of the Dead will take place. The resurrected beings and the matter of the Solar System will be ejected from the reviving Universe (Thaqal / Heavy Mass) to form the Land of Judgment. In the above verse, the 'folded sun' refers to the 'isolated matter of the Solar System.' It will be folded in due time and ejected from the Thaqal to form the Land of Judgment in the Super Space. Section 2 of Chapter 81 [Verse 7-14]: The Final Judgment

When the souls are sorted out. When the female buried alive is questioned: For what crime she was killed? When the scrolls are laid open. When the world on High is unveiled. When the Blazing Fire is kindled to fierce heat. And when the Jannaat is brought near. Each soul shall know what it has put forward.

Remarks:

In this section, the verses are also presented in reverse temporal sequence. The greatest event is described first, followed by a backward movement in time to explain the related events. This structure is likely used to maintain the coherence of the overall surah, although the section refers to the Day of Final Judgment when time will no longer flow in reverse. Therefore, we will discuss the verses in reverse order, starting with the last verse: ―Each soul shall know what it has put forward.‖

Step 1: ―Each soul shall know what it has put forward.‖

A resurrected human will have no memory and will not know any language. After resurrection, his brain will be infused with memory data from the Lawh-Mahfuz, at which point he will know everything. Mentally, he will be the same person as he was on the Earth. Humans will pray for the Judgment, and the marshaling will begin. A written record of deeds (Amal- Nama) will be given to each person, as mentioned in the last verse of this section, ―Each soul shall know what it has put forward‖

Step 2: ―And when the Jannaat is brought near.‖

Next, the Jannaat will approach the Land of Judgment. It will be located in the western super space, beyond the Barzakh, as mentioned in this verse: ―And when the Jannaat is brought near.‖ The Barzakh will be thin at that time, like an astronomical veil. Everyone will feel joy at the sight of the Jannaat, as by then, people will no longer be certain whose religion was correct. Mentally, they will be the same as they were on the Earth.

Step 3: ―When the Blazing Fire is kindled to fierce heat.‖

The galaxies are the objects of Hell. At that time, they will be within the compact universe (Thaqal), but they will be ignited, and the Thaqal will be filled with fire. It will be visible in the super space from the Land of Judgment, as mentioned in this verse: ―When the blazing fire is kindled to fierce heat.‖ Step 4: ―When the world on high is unveiled.‖

Next, the Arsh will descend. The Arsh is the realm above, far larger than the universes. It will be visible above the heads. This is mentioned in the verse: ―When the world on high is unveiled.‖ The Kursi will descend upon the Land of Judgment. From the center of the Land of Judgment, if one looks toward the Kursi, the Jannaat will be on the right, the Thaqal on the left, and the Arsh above the head.

Step 5: ―When the scrolls are laid open.‖

The Arsh holds the CCU (Central Computer of the Universes), and its disc (Lawh-Mahfuz) preserves the record of everything. The CCU operates through Sidratul- Muntaha, and its tentacles will extend over the Land of Judgment to support the trials, as mentioned in the fifth verse from the bottom: ―When the scrolls are laid open.‖

Step 6: ―When the female buried alive is questioned: For what crime she was killed?‖

The Balance will be set, and Allah in form will appear. The Judgment will begin, as mentioned in this verse: ―When the female buried alive is questioned, ―For what crime she was killed?‖‖

Step 7: ―When the souls are sorted out.‖

People will be sorted through Judgment; some will be granted entry into the Jannaat, while others will be destined for Hell, as mentioned in this verse: ―When the souls are sorted out.‖

Section 3 of Chapter 81 [Verse15-18]: A Sign of the Truth

So verily I swear by the receding ships disappear, and the night as it departs, and the dawn as it breathes.

Remarks

The stars are not randomly scattered throughout space; they are organized into systems known as galaxies. In the Quran, a galaxy is referred to as 'Mawaqin- Nujumi.' Here, 'Mawaqi' means 'houses' and 'Nujumi' means 'stars.' Therefore, 'Mawaqin-Nujumi' translates to 'houses of stars,' which refers to galaxies.

―But nay, I swear by the Houses of the Stars (Mawaqin-Nujumi). And, indeed it surely a swear, if you know great‖ [Al Quran 56: 75–76]

In the Quran, a galaxy is called ―sphere‖ as well: ―It is not permitted to the sun to outstrip the moon, nor can the night outstrip the day. And all are in a ‗sphere‘ (Milky Way galaxy) they are floating.‖ [Al Quran 36: 40]

In the above verse, 'falakin' refers to a sphere or domain of space. The sun and the moon are floating within this domain. Therefore, it refers to the Milky Way galaxy. In the verses under discussion, the galaxies are referred to as ships, resembling space-ships that carry stars and other celestial objects: "So verily I swear by the receding ships (space-ships) disappear…" Here, the phrase "receding ships disappear" refers to galaxies that are moving away and gradually fading from view. There are approximately 200 billion galaxies in the visible universe, each containing hundreds of billions of stars that emit light. Light is eternal and cannot be destroyed. If the galaxies were not receding, the light from all the stars would eventually reach the Earth, and every line of sight would end on the surface of a star. The entire sky would appear as bright as the sun. Scientists estimate that, if the galaxies were not receding, the brightness would be forty thousand times greater than the midday sun, resulting in constant, blinding light throughout space—there would be no night, only an unrelenting, sunlit brightness. However, the galaxies are receding, causing them to fade and eventually disappear. As a result, there are dark nights and sunlit days on Earth. A German scientist, Heinrich Wilhelm Matthias Olbers, observed this in 1823. He argued that the darkness of the night contradicts the assumption of a static, infinite, and eternal universe. No one had a satisfactory answer to his argument, so it became known as 'Olber‘s Paradox.' In reality, no one could have imagined that such a vast universe could be expanding! In the 1920s, American scientist Edwin Hubble discovered that the galaxies were receding, providing evidence that the universe was expanding. And, the verses under discussion clearly state that the universe has been expanded to create the darkness of night.

―And the Sky, We constructed it with the hand, and Me for expanders‖ [Al Quran 51:47]

However, in light of the Quran, the universe is currently contracting in diameter from the outermost sky. The universe is shrinking in size, but the space is still expanding due to the rolling of the skies. Probably, for this reason the expansion of the universe is said in the Quran tactfully:

―What! Are ye the more difficult to create or the Sky has constructed it? On high He has raised its canopy, and He has given it order and perfection. Its night does He endow with darkness, and its splendor does He bring out.‖ [Al Quran 79: 27-29] The galaxies are so far apart from one another that by the time the signs of the contracting universe would be visible from Earth, the Sun will have risen from the west.

Verily this is the word of a most honorable Messenger, endued with power, with rank before the Lord of the Arsh, with authority there, faithful to his trust; and your companion is not one possessed. And without doubt he (Muhammad) saw him (Gabriel) in the clear horizon. Neither does he a withholder of the unseen. Nor is it the word of Satan, accursed. Then whither go ye? Verily, this is no less than a Message to the Worlds, to whoever among you wills to go straight. But ye shall not will, except as God wills, the Cherisher of the Universes.
