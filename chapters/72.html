Introduction

The Surah discusses the jinn, including those who belong to the group of Satan (Iblis).

Tafsir of the Surah Section 1 of Chapter 72 [Verse 1-5]: Wonderful Recital

Say: ―It has been revealed to me that a company of jinns listened. They said, ―We have really heard a wonderful recital! It gives guidance to the right, and we have believed therein. We shall not join any with our Lord. And exalted is the majesty of our Lord; He has taken neither a wife nor a son. And that he (Satan / Iblis) used to speak the foolish among us against God an excessive transgression. But, we do think that no man or jinn should say aught that is untrue against God.‖ Remarks:

The jinn are intelligent creatures, and they will face the Final Judgment. They exist around us, but we cannot see or feel them, nor can our instruments detect them. They do not interact with our matter. The Quran states that they are created from 'the poisonous fire of the prairie of fire':

―And the jinn race, We had created before, from the poisonous fire.‖ [Al Quran 15:27]

―And He created Jinns from the prairie of fire‖ [Al Quran 55:15]

All of the above suggests that they are made from anti-matter. This universe is a two-in-one universe, with a parallel anti-universe existing alongside it, inhabited by its anti-creatures. ―We now know that every particle has an anti- particle, with which it can annihilate (In case of the force carrying particles, the antiparticles are the same as the particles themselves). There could be whole anti-world and anti-people made out of anti-particles. However, if you meet your anti-self, do not shake hands! You would both vanish in a great flash of light‖ – A Brief History of Time by Stephen Hawking. I am not suggesting that jinn are our anti-self counterparts, but if the concept of an anti-self is scientifically plausible, then the idea of the jinn is not unscientific. A universe that contains five times more dark matter than ordinary matter should logically harbor creatures made from anti-matter (which is a form of dark matter).

Matter and anti-matter are similar. Anti-matter is composed of anti-particles, while matter is made up of regular particles. When particles and anti-particles encounter each other, they annihilate one another, resulting in the creation of photons. However, particles and anti-particles are protected by force fields—such as the magnetic field associated with an electron or the strong nuclear force field surrounding a proton. Additionally, they may gain protection when forming atoms or anti-atoms. Much is still undiscovered. If Allah has created living creatures from matter and anti-matter, He has also created the systems to ensure their safety. Moreover, atoms and anti-atoms are largely empty. If an atom were compared to a football field, its nucleus would be equivalent to a marble. Thus, matter is transparent to anti-matter. At every moment, a significant amount of anti-matter passes through our bodies, yet we do not feel it. In reality, we do not often observe the collision of matter and anti-matter in nature. Regardless of whether we believe it or not, according to the Quran and Hadith, the jinn exist in this universe. The jinn are capable of moving through space and reaching the stars and planets. Additionally, there are angels assigned to guard their access points in different zones of the skies.

―It is We who have set out fortresses in the Skies and made them fair-seeming to beholders, and We have guarded them from every satans (Jinns) accursed. But any that gains a hearing by stealth is pursued by a flaming fire, bright‖ [Al Quran 15: 16–18]

Section 2 of Chapter 72 [Verse 6-7]: They Think as you Thought

True, there were persons among mankind who took shelter with persons among the jinns, but they (jinns) increased them in folly. And they (jinns) think as you thought that God would not raise up any one.

Remarks:

What the verses mean by, ―True, there were persons among mankind who took shelter with persons among the Jinns‖? The angels guard humans: ―For each there are in succession, before and behind him; they (angels) guard him by command of Allah…‖ [Al Quran 13:11]

Therefore, it is not possible for a satanic jinni to defeat the angels and possess a human. However, since humans are under trial, the satanic jinn are allowed to whisper. However, if a human is an unbeliever and worships idols, the angels do not stop the jinn. In this case, the human may be possessed by a satanic jinn. Allah has made them allies to one another. This could be the way of 'seeking shelter among the jinn,' as the person is escaping the protection of the angels.

―A man is like a horse, whose back never remains vacant, either Allah is riding on him, or a satan.‖ [Hadith]

The Hadith tells us that the main Satan (Iblis) has many followers among the jinn, each of whom is called a satan.

"After each human there is a satan, who is a bad jinn." [Hadith]

Iblis, the leader of the satans, has his throne in the ocean. At the end of each day, all of his followers (satan jinns) report to him. It should be noted that not all jinn are followers of Iblis; there are many good and pious jinn as well. Causes of Following Iblis

Why do some jinn follow Iblis and provoke humans to commit sinful deeds? The jinn are made of anti-matter from a different dimension, yet they have the ability to observe:

―…Verily, he (Iblis) and his tribe (satan jinns) watch you from a position where you cannot see them…‖ [Al Quran 7:27]

A jinni can possess a pagan and enter the human dimension. We know that gravitational force attracts both matter and anti-matter equally. A human's nafs (soul) is a combination of unknown force fields, some of which, like gravitational force, may interact with anti-matter. A jinni connects its nafs with a human's nafs, sharing emotions, enjoying feelings, and perceiving senses. Thus, a jinni can possess a human.

―One day will He gather them all together: "O ye assembly of jinns! Certainly, you have many of the mankind." Their friends among men will say: "Our Lord! Some of us were enjoyed by others, but we reached our term, which Thou did appoint for us." He will say: "The fire be your dwelling- place; you will dwell therein forever, except as God wills." For thy Lord is full of wisdom and knowledge.‖ [Al Quran 6:128] Thus, a jinni ventures into the human dimension through a human's nafs and body, experiencing human life. If the human is eating a fruit, the occupying jinni also enjoys the experience of eating.

―Said: Go thy way; if any of them follow thee, verily hell will be the recompense of you—an ample recompense. And arouse those whom thou can among them with thy voice; make assaults on them with thy cavalry and thy infantry; mutually share with them wealth and children; and make promises to them, but Satan promises them nothing but deceit‖ [Al Quran 17: 63-64]

A jinn possessing a human gradually becomes deeply engraved in the human's nafs and body, causing their thoughts to merge. Their thoughts begin to align with each other, as mentioned in the verses under discussion, ―True, there were persons among mankind who took shelter with persons among the jinns, but they (jinns) increased them in folly. And they (jinns) think as you thought that God would not raise up any one.‖ A human's soul (nafs) plays a crucial role in resurrection. On Earth, the nafs is like a cast, and the body is like a mold. If a jinni possesses a human, the cast (nafs) becomes deformed. At the time of death, the nafs is fixed in a devil-human shape and programs. On the Day of Judgment, the person will be resurrected in this devil- human shape and will be identifiable only by their marks:

―Even if the wrongdoers had all that there is on earth and as much more would they offer it for ransom from the pain of the penalty on the Day of Judgment! But something will confront them from God, which they could never have counted upon—and will become apparent to them satan, what they earned, and will surround them what they used to mock!‖ [Al Quran 39: 47-48]

A bewitched human performs evil activities in collaboration with the mounted jinn. Women, in particular, are vulnerable. A pagan woman may be bewitched without her knowledge. If she feels hatred toward someone and desires their harm, the mounted jinn may share the same sentiment and cause harm in reality. However, a jinni, created from anti-matter, cannot harm a human directly. Instead, he causes harm through the nafs of the possessed person, further corrupting her nafs. However, the woman should not be killed. She should be converted to Islam immediately. As soon as a person embraces Islam, the guardian angels return and drive out the mounted jinn. Afterward, the jinni is only allowed to whisper. A Muslim may view this as a fairy tale, because in Muslim societies, a woman never becomes a witch. However, it is common in pagan societies, where many women are accused and killed for it. A jinni cannot possess a believer in one God, but he can whisper. The whisper creates a sensation in the believer's nafs, which in turn produces a thought in the mind.

―Say: I seek refuge with the Lord and Cherisher of mankind, the King of mankind, the God of mankind, from the mischief of the whisperer who withdraws, who whispers into the chest (mind) of mankind, among jinns and among men.‖ [Al Quran 114: 1-6]

Section 3 of Chapter 72 [Verse 8-10]: Worry about Humans

And we pried into the secrets of sky, but we found it filled with stern guards and flaming fires. We used indeed to sit there in stations to a hearing, but any who listen now will find a flaming fire watching him in ambush. And we understand not whether ill is intended to those on earth, or whether their Lord intends to guide them to right conduct.

Remarks:

The jinn, discussed in the above verses, predicted two plausible causes for additional security: either a great punishment would come to the Earth, or a Prophet of God would appear. In Section-9 of Chapter-6, we discussed how the angels monitor the materialization of fates. They arrive at the Command Stations of the Skies in groups spanning a thousand years, and remain in the Fortresses (star / star- like object) for the time of subsequent move. Later, the angels draw closer to the Earth in smaller groups by Sakinah. From the Sakinah, an angel moves to its job station. Thus, it is possible to know the future from the Command Stations, Fortresses, or Sakinah. The jinn know the future and relay it to the foretellers. Pharaoh learned about the arrival of Moses and responded by killing the newborn male children of the Jewish community for a certain period. Similarly, the Roman Emperor became aware of the arrival of Jesus Christ and ordered the killing of many children. However, the jinns could not know about the arrival of Prophet Muhammad (pbuh). The locations of the angels were strongly guarded, as the verses indicate: ―We used indeed to sit there in stations to a hearing, but any who listen now will find a flaming fire watching him in ambush.‖ The entire Quran was descended together into the First Sky. It was preserved in a Server of the Command Station, with the related angels sheltered in a nearby Fortress. In due time, both the verses of the Quran and the angels were sent near the Earth by a Sakinah. From the Sakinah, the Quran was revealed to Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) in small parts. For this purpose, the movement of the jinn was restricted at specific points in the sky. If the jinn could see Gabriel or the angels carrying the verses, they would easily understand that a Prophet (pbuh) was soon to come. They would inform the foretellers, and the foretellers would then relay it to the Persian and Roman Byzantine emperors, who would jointly order the killing of Arab babies during the predicted period. Even in the Holy Bible, which is full of prophecies, there is no clear indication of the time of Prophet Muhammad's (pbuh) birth. The Bible only mentions that he would come from the desert and that his name would be Ahmad; it also praises him. The Jews of Madinah knew that the Last Prophet would come to Madinah, but they were unaware of where and when he would be born. The jinn are concerned about the humans of their side, as revealed by the word, ―And we understand not whether ill is intended to those on earth, or whether their Lord intends to guide them to right conduct‖ Why they are concerned? Allah has created humans as His vicegerents, but they are not vicegerents of God over one another; they are vicegerents over the lands:

―Behold, thy Lord said to the angels, "Indeed, I am going to place in a land (ardi) a vicegerent." They said, "Wilt Thou place therein one who will make mischief therein and shed blood? While we do celebrate Thy praises and glorify Thy holy?" He said, "I know what ye know not." [Al Quran 2:30]

The planet Earth is a land, Mars is a land, and Jupiter is a land; the lands are scattered throughout the universes, the Samawaat, and the Jannaat. Allah will place a human in each land, as the above verse states: "Indeed, I am going to place in a land a vicegerent." Most likely, He will place one human in each group of lands, such as a galaxy. There are approximately 170 billion large galaxies in the visible universe. Humans are the prime users of the lands. To an angel, the lands are not necessary. For the jinns, the lands of dark matter may be necessary, but not the lands of baryonic matter. Has Allah created this vast universe for nothing? He planned to create humans fifty thousand years before the creation of the universe. He did not evolve the universe without a just purpose. ―We created not the Skies and the Lands and all between them but for just ends. And the Hour is surely coming. So, overlook with gracious forgiveness‖ [Al Quran 15:85]

Thus, a human is meant to be a vicegerent of God over a galaxy in this universe (Samawaat), or over a group of peaceful objects in the Jannaat. No object achieves completeness without a human being. A galaxy is a vast creation, and it has the right to have a vicegerent of Allah:

―The Day We will ask hell (Samawaat / this Universe), ―Are you filled to the full? It will say, are there any more?‖ [Al Quran 50:30]

The jinns are suited for the Samawaat (this universe). The Samawaat is their natural habitat. They are expected to remain here forever, even after the Final Judgment. They may find their rewards in the Samawaat, and there may also be places of punishment for them. Human beings are from the Jannaat and are expected to return there. However, a part of mankind will remain in the Samawaat as vicegerents of God over the galaxies. They will experience pain due to the inherent nature of the Samawaat, which is violent by nature. However, they will evolve, and one day, they will become effective vicegerents of God in their galaxies.

―…I will fill the Hell with jinns and men all together‖ [Al Quran 11:119] ―…I will fill the Hell with jinns and men all together‖ [Al Quran 32:13]

Who would not honor a vicegerent of God, even if he is in punishment? He is entrusted with a domain, a full galaxy. He is one who can call upon God and ask for what he may need. And if God listens to anyone, it is him, for he is God's vicegerent over the galaxy. Other creatures can only praise God, not call upon Him for something. Therefore, the good, the bad, and the ugly jinns are in a mode to help humans, as seen in the verses under discussion, reflecting how deeply concerned they are: ―We used indeed to sit there in stations to a hearing, but any who listen now will find a flaming fire watching him in ambush. And we understand not whether ill is intended to those on earth, or whether their Lord intends to guide them to right conduct.‖ Finally, their concern matters little; what truly matters is how much Allah is concerned.

Section 4 of Chapter 72 [Verse 11-15]: Finishing with the Jinns

There are among us some that are righteous, and some the contrary; we follow divergent paths. But we think that we can by no means frustrate God throughout the lands, nor can we frustrate Him by flight. And as for us, since we have listened to the guidance, we have accepted it; and any who believes in his Lord has no fear either of a short, or of any injustice. Among us are some that submit their wills (to God), and some that swerve from justice. Now those who submit their wills, they have sought out of right conduct; but those who swerve they are fuel for hell-fire.

Section 5 of Chapter 72 [Verse16-28]: Preaching of Islam in Makkah

If they had remained on the Way, We should certainly have bestowed on them rain in abundance that We might try them by that. But if any turns away from the remembrance of his Lord, He will cause him to undergo a severe penalty. And the places of worship are for God. So, invoke not any one along with God; yet when the devotee of God stands forth to invoke Him, they just make round him a dense crowd. Say: I do no more than invoke my Lord, and I join not with Him any. Say: It is not in my power to cause you harm, or to bring you to right conduct. Say: No one can deliver me from God, nor should I find refuge except in Him unless I proclaim what I receive from God and His Messages; and any that disobey God and His Apostle, for them is Hell; they shall dwell therein forever. At length, when they see that which they are promised, then will they know who it is that is weak in helper and least important in point of numbers. Say: I know not whether which you are promised is near or whether my Lord will appoint for it a distant term. He knows the Unseen, nor does He make any one acquainted with His mysteries, except an apostle whom He has chosen. And then He makes a band of observer march before him and behind him that He may know that they have brought and delivered the messages of their Lord, and He surrounds that are with them and takes account of every single thing.
